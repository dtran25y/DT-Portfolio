Escape Flood 3 :

Made in: Unity
Production Time: 3 Weeks
Role: Graphics / VFX / Gameplay Programmer

Project Outline:


This "personal" project is a timed 3D platformer that is meant to challenge the player to go faster, as water rises from the ground up, where you must reach the highest point in the level in order to not drown.

The overall project is meant to build a stronger portfolio (with one Teacher-led project and one project of my own) that will help prepare me for the industry by researching jobs and the kinds of skills they require in their description.

"Escape Flood 3" is a mini-project that is built in mind with developing the skills I lack strength in. Therefore, the week prior to production was spent looking into the job market, until I started ideating my personal project during production which was based on my own research into the job market and what kind of role I wanted to get into from the gaming industry, which was a blend of gameplay programming and graphics programming as well, using node graphs and HLSL.

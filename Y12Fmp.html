The Immune Who Consume :

Made in: Unity
Production Time: 8-10 Weeks
Role: Programmer / Creative Director

Project Outline:


'The Immune Who Consume' is a 2D dungeon-crawler inspired RPG with a story explicating and bringing attention to childhood vaccination and its effectiveness.

Within the game, you go through the body of a child as a white blood cell, in order to fight common illnesses that could be prevented with the help of vaccination, which require the player to collect throughout the bloodstream and strengthen the cells.

The brief for this project was a 'Game Beyond Entertainment', in response to the BAFTA's Game award category which focuses more heavily on delivering emotional impacts or social messages from real-world issues above pure entertainment.

This project involved myself and two 2D Artists (One in environment and another in characters), where we all ideated concepts for this brief with mine ending up as the decided proposition. These ideas were great at finding something both fun and important to tell a message, such as a game where the player is now legally and most follow sound ques that are visualised by waves, guided by the help of strangers to show the struggles of blind people and how important it is for us to help eachother.

However, my idea of showing how important it is for children to be vaccinated endeded up being chosen, with a game loop centered around Max and his family in their visits to the hospital for each vaccine injection to which the white blood cells (player) destroy inside the body to build an immunity to each virus and prevent infection.

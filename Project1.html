Asteroids but it's a roguelike

Production Time: 2-4 Weeks

This project was made in response to a college brief about creating two mini-project, one of an arcade game with a unique change to gameplay as well as a simple racing game in order to work with Unity's New Input system more confidently. This mini-project successfully utilises both keyboard and controller inputs to mobilise the ship and attack various asteroids.

My twist on a classic arcade game was to turn 'Asteroids' into a game about progressively collecting upgrade cards every level ,which would get exponentially harder, with the player having been able to keep some of their cards on a complete loss in order to keep progressing higher and higher on each attempt.
